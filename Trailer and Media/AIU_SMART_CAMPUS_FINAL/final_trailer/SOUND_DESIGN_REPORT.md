# Sound design report (narrated version)
Voice-over: user-supplied ElevenLabs file; its licence/commercial rights are the user's responsibility. Processing: high-pass, light compression, loudness normalisation.
Music and SFX: **generated procedurally by our own scripts** (`audio_lib.py`, `make_mix_vo.py`: numpy/scipy pads, plucks, filtered noise, convolution reverb). No third-party audio, samples or downloads, hence no licence obligations. Music is ducked from the VO envelope; speech-to-bed ratio ≈ 11.9 dB. SFX: title impact, verification ticks, granted chime, lock/handle/door, camera callout, coverage sweep, pipeline and roster ticks, architecture ticks, transitions, closing chime.
Mix: −16.1 LUFS integrated, sample peak −4.4 dBFS. **Not listened to** by me; levels verified numerically only.
